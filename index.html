Mission milestones

From Earth to other worlds

A few dates that help tell the story.

1969
Apollo 11 lands on the Moon. Its descent stage remains at the landing site.
1971
Apollo 15 deploys a lunar laser retroreflector experiment.
1997
Mars Pathfinder lands and deploys Sojourner, the first rover on Mars.
2021–22
Perseverance lands; Ingenuity later photographs parts of its landing system on Mars.

Our mission

Why remember space hardware?

Objects left behind can be scientific instruments, mission hardware, or evidence of how exploration works. Their stories help us understand engineering, discovery, and our responsibility as explorers.
